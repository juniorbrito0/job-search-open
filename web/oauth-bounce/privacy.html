Job Search: privacy notice

Job Search is a private, self-hosted tool that one household uses to look for jobs for a family member. It runs on a computer in that home and is not offered to the public. It is run by Junior Brito, Guelph, Ontario, Canada, who is responsible for this information. Last updated 2026-10-11. If this notice changes, this date is updated.

What it reads

When the person using it presses "Connect Gmail", Google asks them to allow read-only access to their Gmail. Google's read-only permission technically covers the whole mailbox. The tool only searches for job-alert emails (for example from LinkedIn) and messages about job applications, and reads the sender, recipient, subject, date, text and links of those messages. It also reads the Gmail address, to show which account is connected. It does not look through other email.

What it does with it

  • It uses that email only to find job postings and to update the status of applications on the person's own job-search page.
  • It cannot send, delete, label or change any email.
  • Email content and the access key Google issues are kept on the home computer that runs the tool. The only exception is that email text is sent to Anthropic for processing, as described below. This information is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.
  • To read and summarize email, the tool sends the text of relevant emails to Anthropic's Claude AI service, based in the United States, which processes it to do the task. The tool uses a personal Claude subscription with the setting that lets Anthropic use conversations to improve its models turned off. Anthropic may keep this content for a limited time under its own privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Emails may include names and contact details of employers' staff. These are used only to track the application.
  • No person reads the email except the user, or Junior Brito when the user asks for help fixing a problem.
  • The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping it

The person can press "Disconnect" on their job-search page, or remove "Job Search" at myaccount.google.com/permissions, at any time. Either one stops the tool reading Gmail right away. Disconnect also deletes the access key from the home computer. Job details already taken from email stay on the person's job-search page until the job search ends or they ask for them to be deleted, which happens within 30 days of the request.
